Research & privacy agreement

PROMETHEUS is a research prototype. It organizes NASA and related public scientific sources; it is not a safety certification or a substitute for a qualified fire-safety engineer.

Local processing is enabled by default. Conversation memory stays on this device. Selected JSON files are read locally and are not uploaded by PROMETHEUS.

Cloud AI is optional. It is disabled until you explicitly enable it. If enabled, your question and the minimum evidence context needed for the answer are sent to the configured AI service.

Updates are user-controlled. PROMETHEUS may check a public update manifest when you request it; installation of a new APK always requires your confirmation.

By continuing, you accept the research-prototype agreement and local processing terms. You can review privacy, AI, data, and update controls at any time.
