# PROMETHEUS — demo path

1. Landing — introduce PROMETHEUS and the NASA PSI provenance-tracked seed corpus.
2. Universe — visualize experiment records without inventing missing measurements.
3. SAFFIRE-I — open S1 and S2 and show their NASA PSI source and DOI.
4. Compare — contrast concurrent and opposed flow records.
5. Virtual Lab — demonstrate that unsupported quantities remain Unknown rather than being fabricated.
6. Research — show evidence gaps, associations, and limitations.
7. Missions — connect observed evidence to spacecraft, lunar-habitat, and Mars-habitat engineering questions.
8. Data — show investigation provenance and persistent identifiers.
