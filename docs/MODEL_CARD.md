# PROMETHEUS Model Card

## Status

Research prototype / competition software.

## Intended use

Evidence-grounded exploration, comparison, retrieval, analysis, and hypothesis generation for microgravity combustion research.

## Not intended for

- autonomous spacecraft control;
- certified fire-safety decisions;
- emergency response;
- replacing peer review or experimental validation;
- unsupported extrapolation beyond the indexed evidence.

## Known limitations

- incomplete ingestion of the full NASA PSI corpus;
- heterogeneous source formats;
- missing variables;
- small sample sizes in some analyses;
- model uncertainty;
- potential retrieval errors;
- scientific conclusions remain subject to human review.

## Evaluation

The project should maintain benchmark questions with expected evidence, citation correctness, abstention behavior, numerical accuracy, and causal-language tests.
